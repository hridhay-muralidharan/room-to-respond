# Privacy and safety boundaries

## Why privacy matters here

Room to Respond is designed around intimate moments, emotional responses, personal patterns, and reflections a client may want to discuss with a coach. A hosted system may expose requests to application infrastructure and a model provider according to their policies. That creates a trust constraint for a product that works with a person's inner life.

The long-term opportunity is capable language models running privately on personal devices. The README describes the product conditions and go-to-market plan for that opportunity.

## Current prototype

- the guided demo uses fictional, seeded content;
- the reviewer does not enter a personal situation or create an account;
- the demo does not send personal material to a model route;
- the coach-client relationship, authorship, and sharing boundary are shown as product concepts;
- production accounts, permissions, secure sharing, storage, deletion, and recovery are future work.

## Safety boundary

The system is a reflection and exploration product. Therapy, coaching, diagnosis, crisis support, risk assessment, treatment recommendations, and consequential care decisions belong with qualified people and services. The changed situation in the demo is an exercise for reflection, not a prediction or assessment.

The product should operate on the user's words and context. It should not infer clinical states from text, voice, pauses, accent, or emotional tone. Model suggestions should use plain, non-clinical language and remain open to correction.
